About the Journal

Log your actual trades here — symbol, side, and real dollar P&L — separate from the Backtest page, which is for testing a strategy's edge with model/R:R/win-loss data.

Use the account switcher at the top to keep separate journals for separate funded accounts or prop firms. Each account has its own trade list, stats, and CSV export — switch any time, and + New account to add another.

Click + Add Trade to log one manually, or Import CSV to bulk-import from a broker export into the active account. P&L is always the number you (or your broker export) enter — it's never auto-calculated from entry/exit, since that would need a per-instrument point value this page doesn't have.

The trade form opens in Quick Log — Symbol, Date, Side, P&L, Grade, Strategy, and screenshots, with keyboard shortcuts (L/S for side, 1–5 for grade). Switch to Log for Market, Entry/Exit/Lots/Stop/Target, emotion tags, a title, and notes.

The Strategy field is a picker, not free text: describe a setup once (entry conditions, invalidation rules, min R:R) and quickly tag trades to it afterward, instead of retyping an inconsistent name each time. + New strategy from the picker to create one.

Click Show trade details in Log mode to add Entry, Exit, Lots, Stop loss, and Take profit. Stop + Target feed the Avg R:R stat; P&L feeds everything else, including Profit Factor (gross wins ÷ gross losses).

Click any row in All Trades to open it back up, tag how you felt, write notes, and attach up to 4 screenshots. Use the search box and Filters to narrow the list by symbol, side, market, outcome, or date, and the checkboxes to bulk-delete.

Trade numbers sync across devices when you're signed in. Screenshots are kept only in this browser and won't follow you to another device.

Daily shows each day's trades as cards — grade, side, P&L, screenshot, and a Share button, right in the list. Weekly and Strategy roll P&L up by week or by the strategy tag you gave each trade.
