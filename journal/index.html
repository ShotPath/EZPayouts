About the Journal

Log your actual trades here — symbol, direction, and real dollar P&L — separate from the Backtest page, which is for testing a strategy's edge with model/R:R/win-loss data.

Use the account switcher at the top to keep separate journals for separate funded accounts or prop firms. Each account has its own trade list, stats, and CSV export — switch any time, and + New account to add another.

Click + Add new trade to log one manually, or Import CSV to bulk-import from a broker export (looking for columns like Date, Symbol, Side, and P&L) into the active account.

Click any row in Trades History to open it back up — give it a title, write notes on what happened, and attach up to 4 screenshots of the chart.

Trade numbers sync across devices when you're signed in. Screenshots are kept only in this browser and won't follow you to another device.

Use the Daily, Weekly, and Strategy tabs to see P&L rolled up by day, by week, or by the strategy tag you gave each trade.
