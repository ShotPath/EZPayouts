Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

  • We store the trades, strategies, and notes you log so they sync across your devices — trade screenshots stay on your own device only, they're never uploaded.
  • Your password is hashed, never stored or visible in plain text. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we never see that password at all.
  • We don't sell data, and we don't run ad trackers or analytics scripts on this site.
  • If you use the AI Coach feature, whatever you send it (messages, your trade stats, screenshots you attach) goes to Anthropic (Claude) to generate a reply. Nothing else on the site does this.
  • Everything runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure. That's it — Cloudflare, and Anthropic only when you use the Coach.

1. Who this covers

This policy covers ezpayouts.xyz and its subdomains (together, "EZPayouts," "we," "us"). EZPayouts is an independent trading-journal and prop-firm-toolkit product, currently free, built and operated by a solo developer ("ShotPath"). It is not affiliated with any prop firm, broker, or exchange.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Username & password. If you create an account with a username and password, the password is salted and hashed (PBKDF2, SHA-256) before it's ever written to storage — we don't store it, log it, or have any way to recover the original value.
  • Google / Apple sign-in. If you sign in with Google or Apple instead, we receive a signed token from that provider confirming your account's unique ID and (if the provider includes it) a verified email address. We verify that token's signature directly against Google's or Apple's own public keys — your Google/Apple password never passes through EZPayouts.

Trading data you create

  • Trades you log in the Journal or Backtest pages — symbol, direction, P&L, strategy tags, notes, grades, and similar fields.
  • Your selected prop-firm plan/account size in the Lucid cockpit.
  • Screenshots are the one exception — any image you attach to a trade is kept only in your browser's local storage on that device. It is never uploaded to our servers and will not appear if you sign in on a different device or browser.

AI Coach conversations

The Coach chat widget (Backtest page) is opt-in — it only activates when you open it and send a message. When you do, your message, a summary of your recent trade stats, and any screenshot you attach in that conversation are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. We rate-limit this per account per day mainly to control our own API costs, not to profile usage.

Automatically collected technical data

Like any web request, ordinary technical metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamps) passes through Cloudflare's network to serve the site and APIs. We don't run any separate analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and we don't maintain our own server access logs beyond what Cloudflare retains operationally.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account, authenticate you, and keep your session signed in.
  • To sync your trades, strategies, and settings across the devices you sign into.
  • To generate AI Coach responses, only when you actively use that feature.
  • To enforce basic abuse/cost controls (e.g. the Coach's daily message cap).

We do not use your trading data to train any AI model, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.

5. Security & honesty about where we are today

We take reasonable, standard precautions: passwords are salted and hashed rather than stored in plain text, all traffic runs over HTTPS, and OAuth sign-ins are cryptographically verified server-side. That said, EZPayouts is presently a solo-built, pre-revenue product — it has not undergone a third-party security audit, and we don't yet offer things like rate-limited login attempts or two-factor authentication on password accounts.

As the product matures toward a paid tier, hardening account security (audit logging, stronger session controls, 2FA, and a formal security review) is an explicit priority before we'd ask anyone to trust us with payment information. This section will be updated as that work lands.

6. Your choices

  • Access or export. Your trade data is visible to you directly in the app at any time.
  • Correct or delete a trade. You can edit or delete individual trades yourself from the Journal or Backtest page.
  • Delete your account. Full account deletion isn't yet self-service in the app. Email us (below) and we'll delete your account record and synced data promptly, by hand, until that's automated.
  • Local data. Clearing your browser's local storage/site data removes your session and any locally-kept screenshots from that device.

7. Children's privacy

EZPayouts is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes (e.g. a new data use, or the introduction of paid plans) will be called out clearly, not buried in a routine edit.
