Questions

Good to know.

How do I install the download?

Unzip it into a folder you'll keep (Documents is fine). Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and pick that folder. Then pin the EZ icon from the puzzle-piece menu and open a TradingView chart.

To update later: unzip the new download over the same folder, then click the reload arrow on the extension's card in chrome://extensions.

I paid. How do I turn Pro on in the extension?

Click the EZ icon, then Go Pro at the top. The Pro page opens, sees you're signed in and connects your account to the extension. Pro turns on right away.

Is it safe?

It only changes how the chart looks. It doesn't read your symbols, orders or account, and it never trades.

Does it work on my prop firm's platform?

On many sites that use TradingView's charts: open the site, click the EZ icon, then Turn on here. Some firms don't allow browser extensions on evaluation or funded accounts, so check your firm's rules first.

It's installed but my chart looks the same.

Refresh the TradingView tab once, then click the EZ icon and make sure the switches are on.
